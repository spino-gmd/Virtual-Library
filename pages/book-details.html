Gru's Revenge

Read on: 06/07/1967

Rating:

★★★★★

Review:

An exemplary tale of determination and pure suspense. Gru protagonizes a thrilling adventure in the search of his lost aura, since it was stolen by Dr. Nefario. The plot twists were as unexpected as a segmentation fault. A must-read for every despicable one.
